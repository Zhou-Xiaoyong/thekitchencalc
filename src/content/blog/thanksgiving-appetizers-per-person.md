## Two O'Clock, the Doorbell, and a Table That Is Already Full

The first guests arrive at 2:00 with a bottle of wine and dinner is at 4:00. You have a cheese board, a shrimp ring, and a tray of deviled eggs.

For ten people that is 3 to 4 bites each. Thirty-five pieces total, not the eighty your shopping list was built for.

The number is small on purpose. Nobody leaves a Thanksgiving table because the appetizers ran short. Plenty of people leave one because they filled up on cheese at two o'clock.

## The 8-Bites-an-Hour Rule Belongs to Game Day

A party where the apps are the meal needs 6 to 8 bites a person in the first hour, then 3 or 4 for every hour after that. That is the figure the [holiday meal planner](/holiday-meal-planner/) uses when you set it to finger food only, and it is correct for a Super Bowl spread where nachos are dinner.

Thanksgiving is a different event. The apps are a holding pattern in front of a plate that lands somewhere between 1,500 and 2,000 calories before pie.

| Arrival to dinner | Bites per person | For 10 guests |
|---|---|---|
| 30 to 45 minutes | 2 | 20 |
| 1 hour | 3 | 30 |
| 2 hours | 3 to 4 | 35 to 40 |
| 3 hours or more | 5 to 6, plus one hot tray | 55 to 60 |

Two hours is the standard gap, which is why 3 to 4 is the number to shop from. A four-hour wait needs a hot item around the three-hour mark: meatballs, a warm dip, or sliders. Cold cheese at hour four is the point where people start asking whether dinner is soon.

## What Each Tray Actually Holds

Per-person amounts, with the supermarket quantities they translate into. Prices are late-November shelf prices at a regular grocery store, not a warehouse club.

| Item | Per person | Buy for 10 | Typical cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheese | 1½ oz | 15 oz, so a 1-lb wedge plus a small round | $14 |
| Crackers | 4 | one 10-oz box, about 60 crackers | $4 |
| Shrimp cocktail | 3 pieces | 2-lb ring, 21/25 count | $18 |
| Deviled eggs | 1½ halves | 18 halves, from 9 eggs | $3 |
| Veggie tray with dip | 4 pieces, 2 tbsp dip | 40 pieces, one 10-oz dip tub | $12 |
| Nuts and olives | ¼ cup total | 2½ cups combined | $7 |
| Meatballs, if serving hot | 3 | 30, one 2-lb bag holds about 40 | $9 |

All five cold apps come to $67 for ten people, or $6.70 a head. Pick three and it drops to roughly $4 a person. A Thanksgiving with three appetizers has never been the dinner anyone remembers as skimpy.

The shrimp is where the math pays. A 21/25 count means 21 to 25 per pound, so a 2-pound ring holds 44 to 50 pieces and covers 14 people at three each. Loose shrimp at the counter runs $16 a pound against $9 for the frozen ring.

An ounce of cheese is a one-inch cube, so 1½ ounces is a cube plus a slice. Four crackers with that much cheddar is close to 240 calories.

## Build the Board Backwards From the Fridge

Cheese first, because it wants time. Take the wedge out at 1:30 and leave it wrapped loosely on the counter. Brie at 68°F spreads; brie straight from the fridge tears the crackers and tastes like nothing.

Cold items next: shrimp on ice, deviled eggs under a damp paper towel, vegetables standing up so the stems stay crisp. Spoon the dip into a serving bowl at 1:55 and set that bowl inside a larger one with ice.

Hot items last, and only if the gap is over two hours. Meatballs go into a 200°F oven at 2:45, straight from frozen in their sauce, and come out at 3:15 without any attention.

Three types is plenty, four is generous. Five trays for eight people is a fridge full on Wednesday and a counter full on Thursday.

## The Two-Hour Clock on Everything Cold

Shrimp, deviled eggs, deli meat, and dairy dip get two hours at room temperature, then they are done. That is the same 40°F to 140°F rule that governs the turkey, laid out in the [food safety temperature guide](/food-safety-temperature-guide/).

A 72°F kitchen with a crowd in it runs warmer than the thermostat says. Set the dip bowl in ice and it holds the full two hours. Skip the ice and sour cream is warm at the one-hour mark.

Hard cheese is forgiving. Soft cheese, shrimp, and anything with mayonnaise are not. When a tray empties, bring a fresh one from the fridge rather than spooning more onto the warm plate, because the plate itself is now part of the problem.

## If You Already Bought for Twenty

If you bought for twenty and the list shrank, split every tray before it goes out. Half the cheese gets rewrapped, one dip tub stays sealed, the meatballs stay frozen.

Put out half. At 3:15 you will know whether the second half is needed, and nine times out of ten it is not. Setting out everything at 2:00 and watching a $30 cheese board go soft while everyone saves room for turkey does not work.

The shrimp ring is the exception. Once thawed it should be eaten, so buy the 1-pound ring for eight people or fewer and the 2-pound only when the count is real.

## Clear the Table at Quarter Past Three

At 3:15, forty-five minutes before the bird is carved, the filling apps come off the table. Olives, nuts, and pickles can stay. Cheese, shrimp, deviled eggs, and anything with bread goes back into the fridge on a covered plate.

A 45-minute gap does more for a Thanksgiving appetite than any appetizer strategy, and it costs nothing. It also puts the kitchen back in your hands for the last stretch, when the gravy needs whisking and the [rolls need twelve minutes at 425°F](/blog/how-many-rolls-per-person-thanksgiving/).

If dinner is running late, do not refill the apps. Move dinner's start time by twenty minutes instead. The [oven schedule](/blog/thanksgiving-oven-schedule/) is easier to shift than a crowd that has already eaten.

## FAQ

### How many appetizers per person should I plan for Thanksgiving?

Three to four bites per person when dinner is about two hours after arrival, so 35 to 40 pieces for ten guests. That is one cheese plate, one cold item, and one tray of deviled eggs or vegetables. Go to five or six bites only if the wait runs past three hours, and add a hot tray at that point.

### How much cheese per person for a Thanksgiving cheese board?

One and a half ounces per person when there are other apps on the table, which is 15 ounces for ten people. If cheese is the only thing you are serving, plan on 3 ounces each and buy close to two pounds. Three cheeses cover it: one firm, one soft, one sharp or blue for the people who want it.

### How long can Thanksgiving appetizers sit out before they are unsafe?

Two hours at room temperature, and one hour if the room is above 90°F. Shrimp, deviled eggs, and dairy dips are the items to watch, since hard cheese will hold longer without anyone getting sick. Anything past two hours should be thrown out rather than returned to the fridge.
